Devghat Dham Cleaning Sewa by Narayangarh Youth

Narayangarh, Nepal – On Saturday, 2082 Saun 3

On Saturday, 2082 Saun 3, a cleaning service was successfully completed around Devghat Dham under the leadership of the youth of Sri Sathya Sai Centre Narayangarh. A total of 24 volunteers, including Pre-Youth, Mahila Sairam, and youths, were present for the service.

The service covered the area around the toilet constructed by Sri Sathya Sai Centre Bharatpur.

The service began with chanting Omkar and Brahma Gayatri three times and concluded with a Shanti Path.
