Narayan Sewa by Sai Youths

Khurkhure 7, Parsa – On Ashar 22, 2082

Parsa, Nepal – Eight young volunteers from the Sri Satya Sai Sewa Pradesh Samiti 3B provided essential assistance to a family in Parsa Khurkhure 7 on Sunday, Ashar 22, 2082 (B.S.).

The volunteers extended their support to Narayan "Kalpana Bote and Rambahadur Bote", who were in need of aid. Their efforts included installing a tarpaulin sheet to protect the family's home from water intrusion, ensuring their living space remains dry and secure.

In addition to safeguarding their home, Sewa dal also provided food grains as part of their "Anna Seva" (food service) initiative, addressing the family's immediate nutritional needs.

The initiative reflects the ongoing commitment of the Sri Satya Sai Sewa Pradesh Samiti to community welfare and service.
