Kripa Smaran Diwas - 2025

Sri Sathya Sai Center Narayangarh – On Saturday, 2082 Bhadau 7

Sai youth of Narayangarh celebrated Kripa Smaran Diwas on the auspicious occasion of Kushey Aushi,

that brought together a total of 93 devotees (27 male and 66 female.) The program,

which was a offering at Thy lotus feet, commenced with the Ved chanting.

This was followed by a musical offering.Program concluded after the Mangal Arati,

A video of the program is available for viewing on the Saiyouth Narayangarh YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@SaiyouthNarayangarh.

Jai Sai Ram 🙏
